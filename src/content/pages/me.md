## About

I have spent 25 years making software, nearly 15 of them focused on native mobile applications. Today I work independently through Side Effect, helping teams design mobile systems that are easier to understand, test, and change.

I have worked in large organizations where rigor matters and in smaller ones where curiosity and experimentation can move quickly. I enjoy both. What interests me most is the point where an architectural idea has to survive real deadlines, real users, and a real team.

The first half of my career gave me a strong foundation in Java, object-oriented programming, and web technologies. Then I discovered Swift in 2015 and immediately enjoyed the new set of ideas it opened up. Functional programming, explicit state, and controlled side effects now shape much of how I approach safety, testability, and scale.

I have also spent years building and mentoring native mobile teams. A recurring part of that work has been bringing iOS and Android developers together around shared engineering principles without pretending the two platforms are identical.

Outside client work, I contribute to the Swift community through projects including [RxFlow](https://github.com/RxSwiftCommunity/RxFlow), [Feedbacks](https://github.com/CombineCommunity/Feedbacks), [AsyncStateMachine](https://github.com/sideeffect-io/AsyncStateMachine), [AsyncExtensions](https://github.com/sideeffect-io/AsyncExtensions), and Apple’s [Swift Async Algorithms](https://github.com/apple/swift-async-algorithms).

When I am away from a keyboard, I forge knives, fly drones, and ride along the coast near my home in Normandy.
