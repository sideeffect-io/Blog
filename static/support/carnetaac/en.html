Carnet AAC brings together the dashboard, driving goals, monthly trips, learners, family vehicles, GPS tracking, CarPlay, statistics and useful reminders in a local-first interface, with iCloud only when the user enables it.

Program and goals

Choose the main unit, distance or duration, keep the France AAC preset or adjust milestones. The last milestone becomes the logbook final target.

Aligned stats

Home screen widget

Complete export

Minimal GPS

Support

A question, bug or request?

Support covers issues with the app, goals, GPS tracking, CarPlay, Live Activities, the widget, iCloud, PDF, CSV and JSON exports, and data deletion.

How does family sharing work?

Each learner has their own logbook. A family can create multiple logbooks on one iPhone, share a logbook through iCloud with invited accounts, or keep a logbook unshared.

Can I track something other than 3,000 km?

Yes. Carnet AAC offers the France AAC preset in kilometers and also lets you track duration with customizable milestones. This setting is used by the dashboard, statistics, widget and exports.

What should I include in a support request?

Include the iPhone model, iOS version, Carnet AAC version, iCloud status if sharing is involved, and the steps needed to reproduce the issue.

Does GPS replace kilometer validation?

No. GPS provides an estimate. The user validates or corrects kilometers before manual saving; if automatic stop is enabled, the app saves the trip with the available estimate.

What does CarPlay do?

CarPlay provides a simplified interface to choose the learner and vehicle, start a GPS trip, follow distance and duration, then stop and save the trip. Detailed settings and corrections stay in the iPhone app.

What are Live Activities for?

They show the current GPS trip on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island, with distance, duration, tracking status and a stop action when iOS allows it.

What does background GPS tracking mean?

It lets the app continue estimating an explicitly started trip when the iPhone is locked or the app leaves the foreground. It is enabled at first launch, can be disabled before each start, and can allow automatic stop after GPS inactivity.

How do I back up or restore the whole logbook?

The About tab can export a complete JSON file with learners, vehicles and trips, then import a valid Carnet AAC file. Import replaces existing data.

How do I stop iCloud sharing?

Open the logbook sharing screen in the app to manage invited accounts or stop sharing. Device iCloud settings still apply.

How do I delete logbook data?

In the app, open the About tab and use the data deletion action. If iCloud is enabled, deletion can sync through iCloud.
