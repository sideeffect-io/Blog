Support

A question, bug or request?

Support covers the app, goals, trips, GPS, CarPlay, the widget, iCloud, exports and data deletion.

How does family sharing work?

Each learner has their own logbook. A family can create multiple logbooks on one iPhone, share a logbook through iCloud with invited accounts, or keep a logbook unshared.

Can I track something other than 3,000 km?

Yes. ViaPad supports the 3,000 km target and custom kilometer or driving-hour goals.

Does GPS replace kilometer validation?

No. GPS provides an estimate that you can validate or correct before saving.

What does CarPlay do?

CarPlay lets you choose the learner and vehicle, start a GPS trip, follow distance and duration, then stop and save.

How do I back up or restore the whole logbook?

The About tab can export a complete JSON file, then import a valid ViaPad file.

How do I stop iCloud sharing?

Open the logbook sharing screen in the app to manage invited accounts or stop sharing. Device iCloud settings still apply.

How do I delete logbook data?

In the app, open the About tab and use the data deletion action. If iCloud is enabled, deletion can sync through iCloud.
